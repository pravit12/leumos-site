This is the first public note for the Leumos AI marketing site. It exists.

What landed:

- **Next.js 15 + App Router**, Tailwind CSS, and TypeScript on Vercel.
- **Lighthouse CI** on every preview deploy with a 90+ floor across performance, accessibility, best-practices, and SEO.
- **Security headers** wired in `next.config.ts`: `X-Content-Type-Options`, `X-Frame-Options`, `Referrer-Policy`, and a tight `Permissions-Policy`.
- A holding-page `/` so we can hand out a real URL while the story site is still being designed.

There is no waitlist sign-up here yet — that ships with the brand and story work. This release is the floor we build on.
