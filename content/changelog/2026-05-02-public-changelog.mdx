You're looking at the first version of the Leumos AI changelog.

What landed:

- A `/changelog` route that lists release notes newest-first.
- A canonical URL for each entry, so any post can be linked or shared.
- An [Atom feed](/changelog.xml) at `/changelog.xml` for anyone who wants to subscribe in a feed reader.
- `BlogPosting` JSON-LD on every entry and the index, so search engines understand what these posts are.
- Sitemap inclusion for the index and every entry.

What's intentionally not here yet:

- Comments, reactions, and email subscriptions on individual entries.
- A public roadmap. We'll bring that back once we have something concrete to commit to.

The cadence will land once we have a Marketing and Content lead. Until then, expect entries here whenever something visible to users actually ships.
